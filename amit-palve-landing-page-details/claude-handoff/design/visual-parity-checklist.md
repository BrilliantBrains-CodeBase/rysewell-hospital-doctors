# Visual Parity Checklist

Use this checklist when Claude recreates the landing page in Rysewell.

## Layout and Section Flow
- Section order matches content-map.md exactly.
- Hero to final CTA narrative flow is preserved.
- Desktop and mobile spacing rhythm is consistent across sections.

## Typography and Hierarchy
- Serif heading style and sans-serif body style maintained.
- Hero headline remains strongest visual element.
- Section titles, body copy, and CTA labels preserve relative hierarchy.

## Color and Surface System
- Primary medical blue tone remains dominant.
- Soft neutral background surfaces for readability.
- CTA buttons maintain strong contrast and clear affordance.

## Component Styling
- Rounded cards, subtle elevation, and clean borders are preserved.
- Navbar has premium fixed treatment with clear CTA action.
- Stats strip and trust bar are visually distinct and scannable.

## Conversion UX
- CTA buttons are visible in navbar, hero, video section, and final CTA.
- Booking modal interaction and transitions remain smooth.
- Floating WhatsApp action remains present and unobtrusive.

## Responsive Behavior
- Hero scales correctly on mobile without clipping.
- Grids collapse logically (cards/services/stats/testimonials).
- Modal remains usable on small screens with proper scrolling.
